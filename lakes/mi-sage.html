Where the data comes from

Bathymetry for Sage Lake is built from the provincial open-data survey record; where soundings are sparse the app marks cells unsurveyed rather than guessing. Source: Michigan DNR Institute for Fisheries Research inland lake contours (Michigan GIS Open Data) with the USGS National Hydrography Dataset outline.
